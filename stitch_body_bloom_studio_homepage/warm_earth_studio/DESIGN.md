## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Core Atmosphere
This design system embodies mindful movement, physical therapy, and restorative bodywork with zero pretentiousness (*"Kobieca ekipa i zero spiny"*). The interface prioritizes calm reassurance, warmth, accessibility, and bodily wellness. It intentionally rejects aggressive fitness tropes—hyper-intense neon accents, gamified pressure counters, intimidating dark-mode aesthetics, or gym-bro bravado. Instead, it invites the user into a sunlit, earthy, boutique sanctuary that feels as grounding and restorative as a warm herbal tea post-physiotherapy.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Women and local community members seeking supportive, personalized physiotherapy, therapeutic massage, pilates, and low-stress group movement in Myślenice.
- **Emotional Response:** Comfort, relief, safety, and agency. The interface feels inviting, organized, deeply human, and effortless to navigate during booking or service browsing.

### Design Movement: Warm Organic Contemporary
A synthesis of warm organic minimalism and tactile hospitality design:
- Unhurried, breathable whitespace with wide, grounded padding.
- Silky, micro-textured backgrounds resembling natural sand and raw oatmeal.
- Soft, generous structural rounding (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-3xl`) mimicking smooth river pebbles and organic ceramics.
- Hairline, low-contrast natural borders resembling fine tailoring or tactile linen stitching.

## Colors

### Palette Philosophy
The palette is derived entirely from natural clay, sun-dried terracotta, dried sage foliage, and textured oatmeal paper. It creates an atmosphere of tactile comfort while maintaining AA/AAA WCAG accessibility for appointments, clinical treatment descriptions, and practitioner bios.

### Color Roles & Tone Values
- **Primary (`#BD5D38` - Warm Terracotta / Clay):** Represents confidence, bodily warmth, and circulation. Used for key call-to-actions (e.g., "Zarezerwuj wizytę"), active state indicators, and focal branding moments.
- **Secondary (`#687B63` - Muted Olive / Sage):** Brings therapeutic serenity, recovery, and botanic balance. Used for status badges (confirmed booking, available slots), physiological recovery tags, and wellness badges.
- **Tertiary (`#D99B7D` - Sunlit Clay / Soft Peach):** Provides supportive highlights, secondary container washes, and delicate card accents.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F7F4EE` - Soft Oatmeal / Raw Sand):** The primary light-mode background. Eliminates harsh display glare, establishing a gentle, linen-like visual resting plane.
- **Deep Espresso (`#24201D`):** The primary text color. Softened from absolute pitch black to eliminate sterile clinical starkness while ensuring pin-sharp contrast against the sand backdrop.
- **Subtle Surface & Line Tones:**
  - Card & Container Fill: `#FFFFFF` and `#F0EAE1`
  - Subtle Border / Divider: `#E3DAD0`
  - Muted Caption & Meta Text: `#6E675F`

## Typography

### Architectural Rationale
The typography is driven by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, a modern humanist geometric grotesque. Its open counters, curved junctions, and soft stroke terminals communicate warmth, approachability, and contemporary precision. It avoids geometric severity while staying readable across clinical terminology, session timetables, and pricing matrices.

### Scaling & Readability
- Display and top-tier headlines feature slightly negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to keep Polish compounding words and titles cohesive.
- Body copy uses generous leading (`1.55` to `1.6`) to provide relaxed readability, preventing visual exhaustion during intake forms and treatment overviews.
- Mobile scale fallbacks (`display-lg-mobile`, `headline-lg-mobile`) keep headings readable on handheld devices without breaking lines abruptly.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Philosophy
The studio's digital experience relies on a fluid, breathable layout with generous breathing room. Visual clutter is treated as digital tension; whitespace is used deliberately to pace the user's journey.

### Grid System & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12-column responsive grid with a maximum content container width of `1240px`, flanked by `margin: 2rem` and column `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column responsive grid with `margin: 1.5rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column responsive grid with `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`.

### Spatial Flow
- Interactive forms, intake steps, and therapist cards avoid dense clustering. 
- Section stack intervals use `space-xl` (48px) to `space-xl * 1.5` (72px) to ensure each service discipline (Physiotherapy, Therapeutic Massage, Group Classes) holds its own dedicated, calm visual plane.

## Elevation & Depth

### Ambient Organic Depth
Depth in this system avoids heavy drop shadows or artificial multi-axis lights. Instead, it relies on soft natural diffusion, delicate tonal layering, and fine organic borders.

### Surface Tiers & Layering
1. **Base Surface (Ground):** Soft Oatmeal (`#F7F4EE`) serves as the foundational room environment.
2. **Elevated Containers (Cards & Modals):** Crisp Pure Linen (`#FFFFFF`) or Warm Sand Tone (`#F0EAE1`) gently resting on the base surface.
3. **Ghost Boundaries:** Outlines in `#E3DAD0` (1px solid) give cards structural definition without stark delineation.
4. **Soft Diffused Cast Shadow:**
   - *Card Rest State:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(36, 32, 29, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(36, 32, 29, 0.02)`
   - *Floating Modal / Popover:* `0 16px 36px -4px rgba(36, 32, 29, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(36, 32, 29, 0.03)`
   - Shadows are tinted slightly with Deep Espresso (`#24201D`) rather than synthetic neutral black, preserving the natural daylight ambience.

## Shapes

### Shape Language & Curvature
The shape language draws inspiration from smooth beach stones, ceramic vessels, and ergonomic studio equipment:
- **Base Components (Inputs, Small Badges):** `0.5rem` (8px) for comfortable touch boundaries.
- **Cards, Service Tiles & Action Sheets:** `1rem` (16px) or `1.5rem` (24px) (`rounded-xl` to `rounded-2xl`) creating a welcoming, non-clinical frame.
- **Pill Elements:** Buttons, status badges, and selectable category tags use full radius pill capping (`rounded-full`) to emphasize friendly softness and finger-friendly targets.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Button:** Filled Terracotta (`#BD5D38`) with pure white text, full pill curvature (`rounded-full`), padded `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Subtle hover state shifts to a rich roasted clay (`#A74E2D`).
- **Secondary Button:** Outlined with delicate `#E3DAD0` or Olive tint (`#687B63`), containing text in Deep Espresso, filling on hover with a warm tinted sand wash (`#F0EAE1`).
- **Text / Ghost Button:** Espresso text with an understated terracotta arrow or dot indicator, minimal padding.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Used for selecting body zones, class types (e.g., *Zdrowy kręgosłup*, *Drenaż limfatyczny*, *Masaż kobido*), and practitioner schedules.
- Unselected: Sand container (`#F0EAE1`), Deep Espresso text, borderless.
- Selected: Soft Sage container (`#687B63`) with white text, or Terracotta outline with matching terracotta text.

### Treatment & Specialist Cards
- Pure linen white fill (`#FFFFFF`), `1.25rem` padding, `1rem` corner radius, surrounded by a 1px `#E3DAD0` boundary.
- Team member cards feature softly framed portrait photography with subtle duotone warmth, accompanied by specialty tags and experience summaries.

### Input Fields & Booking Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1.5px `#E3DAD0`, inner padding `0.875rem 1rem`, text `#24201D`.
- Focus state: Smooth transition to a Terracotta border (`#BD5D38`) paired with an ambient 3px glow ring (`rgba(189, 93, 56, 0.15)`). Never uses aggressive system-blue outlines.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Custom rounded squares (checkbox) and soft concentric discs (radios).
- Active state fills in Muted Sage (`#687B63`) or Terracotta (`#BD5D38`) with a crisp white check or center pip.

### Studio-Specific Components
- **Intake Progress Stepper:** Soft organic pebble indicators showing steps (e.g., *Konsultacja → Wywiad → Plan terapii*).
- **Schedule Ribbon:** Horizontal fluid date scroll with pill-shaped active dates highlighting available morning and evening slots.
- **Vibe Callout Tile:** Calm, full-width oatmeal card featuring quote typography, warm portraits of the team, and reassurance messaging (*"Pierwszy raz na masażu? Pomożemy Ci przygotować się bez stresu"*).
