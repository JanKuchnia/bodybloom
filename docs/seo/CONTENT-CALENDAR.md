# BODY BLOOM: Content Calendar

Cadence: **1 post every 2 weeks** (about 22 in year one) plus the seasonal pages below. Small by design: the owner must be able to keep it up. Every post has one primary keyword, one internal link to a service page, one CTA, and is written or reviewed by the relevant practitioner. Length 600-1000 words. No medical promises.

Validate the keyword ideas in Search Console after 8-12 weeks; swap topics that show no impressions.

## Repurposing from Instagram

The studio already posts regularly. Reuse: each community event becomes a short `/wydarzenia/` entry; each helpful physio tip becomes an expanded blog post; use real photos (with consent) for the site. Do not embed the live Instagram feed (cookies and speed): use static thumbnails with links.

## Quality gates per post

- [ ] A real person's review (trainer/physio) for health topics
- [ ] One unique photo from the studio
- [ ] Answers the question in the first 2 sentences (AI-snippet friendly)
- [ ] Internal link to a service page and to kontakt
- [ ] Title ≤ 60 chars, meta ≤ 155 chars
- [ ] Published date and updated date shown
- [ ] Added to the sitemap, GBP post made
